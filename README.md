## 🌱 About The Project

**NariPreneur** is a Generative AI-powered entrepreneurship assistant created to help women turn their existing skills, ideas, and local opportunities into practical business decisions.

Many women possess valuable skills such as handicrafts, tailoring, food preparation, beauty services, farming, or other local expertise. However, converting those skills into a sustainable business can be challenging without access to simple and accessible business guidance.

NariPreneur aims to bridge this gap by providing AI-assisted guidance around:

* 💡 Business opportunity discovery
* 👥 Customer validation
* 💰 Cost and profit understanding
* 📊 Business idea evaluation
* 🚀 Better business decision-making

The goal is simple:

> **Help women move from "I have a skill" to "I have a business opportunity."**

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Women, especially those from rural and underserved communities, may have valuable skills but face difficulties when trying to convert those skills into sustainable business opportunities.

Common challenges include:

* Lack of accessible business guidance
* Difficulty identifying suitable business opportunities
* Limited understanding of customer demand
* Difficulty calculating real costs and profits
* Lack of confidence in evaluating a business idea
* Limited access to traditional entrepreneurship resources

The problem is therefore not only a lack of skills.

The larger gap is the lack of **simple, accessible, and practical business decision-making support**.

NariPreneur addresses this gap using Generative AI to provide personalized guidance based on the user's business idea, skills, customers, costs, and requirements.

# 💡 Our Solution

NariPreneur works as a **GenAI-powered entrepreneurship assistant**.

The user provides information about their:

* Skills

# ✨ Key Features

## 💡 1. Discover Your Business Opportunity

NariPreneur helps users explore possible business opportunities based on their existing skills and ideas.

The objective is to help users identify how their existing abilities can potentially be converted into a source of income.

---

## 👥 2. Will Customers Actually Buy?

Having a business idea is not enough.

NariPreneur encourages users to think about:

* Who their target customers are
* What their customers need
* What problem their product/service solves
* Whether there is potential demand
* Why customers would choose their product/service

This helps users evaluate an idea from the **customer's perspective**.

---

## 💰 3. Know Your Real Profit

Revenue does not equal profit.

NariPreneur helps users think about:

```text
Revenue
   -
Business Costs
   =
Potential Profit
```

Users can consider:

* Product/service price
* Raw material costs
* Operating expenses
* Other business costs
* Expected sales
* Potential profit

This encourages users to make decisions based on actual costs rather than assumptions.

---

## 📊 4. Better Business Decisions

NariPreneur is designed as a **decision-support assistant**.

It helps users think through:

* Business opportunities
* Customer needs
* Pricing
* Costs
* Profitability
* Potential improvements
* Next steps

The objective is not to make the decision for the entrepreneur, but to help her make a **better-informed decision**.

# 🧠 Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering is an important part of NariPreneur.

The prompts are designed to encourage the model to produce responses that are:

* Simple
* Practical
* Relevant
* Beginner-friendly
* Action-oriented
* Context-aware

### Prompt Design Goals

```text
User Context
     ↓
Understand the Business Idea
     ↓
Identify the User's Need
     ↓
Apply Appropriate Instructions
     ↓
Generate Relevant Guidance
     ↓
Present Simple & Actionable Insights
```

During development, prompts can be refined by reviewing model outputs and improving instructions based on the quality and relevance of the responses.

The hackathon submission specifically requires documentation of significant AI tools/models, important prompts, prompt refinement, and external references where applicable.

Testing is performed using different business scenarios to evaluate the usefulness and relevance of AI-generated responses.

| ⚠️ Unexpected Input | Can it handle difficult or unusual inputs?         |

### Testing Process

For each scenario:

```text
User Scenario
     ↓
Expected Result
     ↓
Run Prototype
     ↓
Actual Result
     ↓
Compare
     ↓
Pass / Fail
     ↓
Improve Prompt / Solution
```

The hackathon evaluation framework requires testing with at least three different scenarios, including one difficult or unexpected input, and documenting expected results, actual results, pass/fail status, and improvements.

# 🛡️ Responsible AI

NariPreneur is designed with responsible AI considerations in mind.

## 🔐 Privacy

User information should be handled responsibly.

The system should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information and should protect information provided by users.

---

## ⚖️ Bias & Fairness

AI-generated recommendations may contain assumptions or biases.

Therefore, recommendations should be treated as guidance and evaluated according to the user's actual situation.

---

## ⚠️ Accuracy

Generative AI can sometimes produce inaccurate or incomplete information.

Important decisions related to:

* Finance
* Investments
* Business registration
* Legal requirements
* Major financial commitments

should be independently verified using reliable information.

---

## 👩‍💼 Human Oversight

NariPreneur is a **decision-support tool**, not a replacement for human judgment.

Users should combine AI-generated insights with:

* Real customer feedback
* Local market knowledge
* Actual cost information
* Real-world experimentation
* Expert advice when required

These considerations align with the hackathon's Responsible AI requirements covering privacy, bias, inaccurate outputs, security, transparency, and human oversight.
